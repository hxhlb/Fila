Fila 0.7.3 fixes a crash in the tab overview, tabs that showed no thumbnail, and what Properties copies.

- Dragging a tab card to a new place in the overview no longer closes Fila (#31).
- On an iOS 16 iPhone every tab card showed a plain block with a folder icon instead of a picture of its page; a page that the system refuses to hand back as it was last drawn is now drawn again for the card (#31).
- In Properties, touching and holding Calculate Checksums or Advanced Information no longer offers to copy the description under it (#32).
- Touch and hold the name at the top of Properties to copy the item's name or its path (#32).
- Touching and holding an extended attribute copies its name rather than its size.

Choose the RootHide or the rootless Debian package for a jailbroken device, the TrollStore TIPA for TrollStore, or the standard IPA to sideload. SHA-256 checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
