Fila 0.7.1 fixes a crash when Properties is opened again and again.

- Opening Properties on a photo repeatedly, for one photo or several, no longer closes Fila on iOS 16 (#30).
- The same change applies to the Save To folder panel and to app icons appearing in a folder's list, which could close Fila the same way.

Choose the RootHide or the rootless Debian package for a jailbroken device, the TrollStore TIPA for TrollStore, or the standard IPA to sideload. SHA-256 checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
