Fila 0.6.10 tidies the bottom bar.

- Search on the Music, Installed Apps and Search screens is a button again on iPad, instead of a field cut off after three letters.
- The space between the bottom bar's buttons and the path bar now matches the space at the bar's edges.
- On a page without Search, such as a file viewer, the path bar fills the empty space on the left.
- A file name too long for the path bar is shown from its beginning, with its icon, instead of from its end.

Choose the RootHide or the rootless Debian package for a device running custom firmware, the TrollStore TIPA for TrollStore, or the standard IPA to sideload. SHA-256 checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
