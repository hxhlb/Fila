Fila 0.7.0 fixes a crash in Properties and makes tab thumbnails readable.

- Properties for a photo, such as a PNG or a JPEG, no longer closes Fila while its preview loads.
- Tab thumbnails are sharp, and show a phone's width of the page at a readable size instead of the whole page shrunk into the card.
- On a right-to-left language, a tab thumbnail shows the side of the page where names start.
- Removing an item from the clipboard with a swipe, or removing missing items, takes the rows off the list at once.
- Swiping to remove a saved server waits for your answer on the confirmation card before anything changes.

Choose the RootHide or the rootless Debian package for a jailbroken device, the TrollStore TIPA for TrollStore, or the standard IPA to sideload. SHA-256 checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
